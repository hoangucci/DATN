7. HIERARCHY RUNTIME MONG DOI
============================================================

Main Camera
  FirstPersonViewmodelRoot
    AttackPivot
      RestPosePivot
        FirstPersonSwordVisual

- AttackPivot: Rest position/scale + animated camera-local offset/rotation.
- RestPosePivot: Rest rotation cua mesh.
- FirstPersonSwordVisual: local position zero, rotation identity, scale one.

Effective rotation = AttackPivot Swing Rotation * RestPosePivot Rest Rotation.
